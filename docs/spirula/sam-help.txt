  C:\Travail\DEV\SfM_Splat_App_26\sfm-splat-app\tools\spirula\spirula.exe sam mask <images> [options]
        Mask the part of every frame that is never scene: a fisheye border, a
        watermark, the rig in shot. Needs no model -- it is in the same place in
        every frame, so it is a shape, not an object.
        --out <dir>             where the masks go (default: 'masks' beside the image
                                directory)
        --shape <spec>          use these shapes instead of looking for a border; ';'
                                separates them and a leading '-' cuts one out again
                                ellipse cx,cy,rx,ry  |  rect x0,y0,x1,y1
                                "ellipse 0.5,0.5,0.49,0.49; -rect 0,0.93,1,1"
        --shrink <f>            pull the found boundary inwards, as a fraction of its
                                radius (default 0.01); the outermost pixels of a lens
                                circle are dim and smeared, and worth losing
        --samples <n>           frames read per camera when looking for the border
                                (default 24)
        --dark <0..255>         brightness at or below which a pixel counts as black,
                                0..255 (default 16)
        --mask-image <file>     also drop wherever this image is black -- the way out
                                when no ellipse or rectangle describes the region
        --preview <file>        write one frame with the mask drawn over it, to check
                                by eye
        --print                 print the shapes and write nothing
        --replace               overwrite masks already in the output folder; without
                                it they are intersected, which is how this stacks on
                                top of a model's masks
